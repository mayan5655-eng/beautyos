## Source and rights

- **Source:** generated by the product owner with an AI image tool (tool name to be filled in here by the
  owner: the brief said only "TOOL"). The people shown are AI-generated; they are not real people.
- **Rights:** the owner confirmed (2026-10-10) that the tool's terms allow commercial use of the images.
- **Files:** the originals were 941 x 1672 JPEGs (260-320 KB); these are resized to 864 px wide, mozjpeg
  quality 80 (110-150 KB each) to stay under the 200 KB asset budget (`test-asset-budget.ts`).

## How they are used

- Each presenter template carries one of these as the slot's `defaultImage` (`kind: 'ai_character'`).
  Until the cosmetician picks a photo of her own, the template shows the character.
- While the character is showing, a fixed label layer ("דמות AI להמחשה", "AI character for illustration") is
  part of the design, inside the reel safe zone, so it is in every export. The label is hidden automatically
  when she uses her own photo in that slot.
- The character is an illustration of the template. The copy is written as an invitation to book, never as
  a testimonial from a real client.
